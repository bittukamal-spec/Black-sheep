Expenses: decimals, product size, and quicker filling

1 · Decimals everywhere money is typed: expenses, bills, repeating expenses, partner money in/out and stock "total paid" all accept ₹155.20. Totals show paise only when there are any (₹1,494 or ₹155.20).
2 · Product size: every expense item gets "how much": a number plus a unit tap (g · kg · ml · L · pcs · pack). The app then shows the price per unit (₹/kg, ₹/L) so you can compare shops, and fills the stock amount for you.
3 · Quicker filling: start typing an item and the app suggests what you bought before. One tap fills its category, size, last price and shop.
