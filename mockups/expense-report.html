What fills the report by itself
✅ Bought: expenses with "+ to stock" (suggestions link them for you)
✅ Used: every sale, through recipes (2 slices per toast)
✅ Expiry: each stock item gets "keeps for N days", so the expiry date is set when you buy
✅ Wasted: one tap on the expiry alert, or Stock → Waste for spills
✋ You do: a 2-minute count on Sundays (optional, fixes small drifts)
New setting per stock item: "Keeps for … days" (bread 3, milk 2, beans 30). Buying sets the expiry by itself, and on that day Admin asks one question.

What you'd set up once

  1. Recipes for toasts and sandwiches (e.g. Avocado toast = 2 slices sourdough + 1 avocado + 10 g butter). Coffee recipes already exist.
  2. Pack sizes and "keeps for" days on stock items (Sourdough: 1 pack = 12 slices, keeps 3 days).
  3. Link each regular purchase to its stock item once. After that, suggestions do it automatically.
