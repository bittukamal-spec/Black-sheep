Expenses: one bill, several items

For a shop trip where you buy many things together (e.g. D-Mart: milk, cups, syrup). Shop, date, who paid and how are filled once; each item has its own name, quantity, price and category. The total adds itself up. In reports each item counts under its own category, so "Milk & dairy" and "Cups" stay correct.
